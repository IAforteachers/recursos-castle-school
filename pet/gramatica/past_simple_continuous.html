Past Simple vs Past Continuous

Practise affirmative sentences, negatives and questions, then challenge yourself with a mixed review. Read the explanations and learn from your mistakes.

Quick grammar guide

Past Simple

Use it for completed actions in the past.

+ I visited my cousins yesterday.
− I didn't visit them last week.
? Did you visit them?

Past Continuous

Use it for actions that were in progress at a specific time in the past.

+ She was reading at 8 p.m.
− She wasn't reading at 8 p.m.
? Was she reading at 8 p.m.?

Using both tenses

I was walking home when it started to rain.

Recuerda: para la negativa del Past Simple usamos didn't + verbo en infinitivo sin to. En las preguntas: Did + sujeto + verbo base. Para el Past Continuous usamos was/were + verbo-ing; en negativa, wasn't/weren't. Atención: when y while aportan contexto, pero no determinan por sí solos qué tiempo verbal hay que usar.
