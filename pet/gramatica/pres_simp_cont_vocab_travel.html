Quick revision

Past Simple: completed actions in the past. We visited the museum yesterday.

Past Continuous: an action in progress at a particular past moment. At 8 p.m., I was reading.

Together: We were walking when it started to rain. The longer action was in progress; the shorter action interrupted it.

Travel vocabulary: book a ticket (reservar un billete), pack a suitcase (hacer una maleta), catch a train (coger un tren), go sightseeing (visitar lugares de interés), boarding pass (tarjeta de embarque).
