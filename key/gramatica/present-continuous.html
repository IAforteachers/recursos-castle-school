Present Continuous

Gramática · Key (A2) · Explicación y práctica

1. Explicación

¿Para qué sirve?

1. Acciones que ocurren ahora mismo:
Look! It is raining.

2. Acciones temporales (esta semana, estos días):
I am studying for my exam this week.

3. Planes ya organizados para el futuro:
I am meeting Anna tomorrow at 5.

¿Cómo se forma?

am / is / are + verbo + -ing
AfirmativaNegativaPregunta
II am workingI am not workingAm I working?
he / she / itShe is workingShe is not (isn't) workingIs she working?
you / we / theyThey are workingThey are not (aren't) workingAre they working?

Ortografía del -ing

ReglaEjemplo
Normal: añade -ingplay → playing, eat → eating
Termina en -e: quita la -emake → making, write → writing
Consonante + vocal + consonante (sílaba corta): duplica la últimarun → running, sit → sitting, swim → swimming

Palabras que suelen acompañarlo

now, at the moment, today, this week, right now, Look!, Listen!

2. Práctica

Contesta y pulsa Comprobar. Puedes usar formas cortas (isn't, 'm).

A. Completa con el verbo en Present Continuous

1. Look! It (rain).
2. I (not / watch) TV now.
3. She (study) for her exam today.
4. They (play) football in the park.
5. We (not / work) today.

B. Elige la opción correcta

6. "Where's Tom?" "He in the kitchen."
7. you listening to me?
8. The children at the moment.
9. I meeting Anna tomorrow at 5. (plan para el futuro)
10. My brother doing his homework now.
