The Missing Trophy

English escape room · Level A2 · Present simple and present continuous

Good morning, detectives!

Something terrible is happening at Oakwood School. The school trophy is missing!

The thief is still in the building. You have 25 minutes to find the thief and open the box.

Solve 4 clues. Every clue gives you ONE number. Put the numbers in order to open the box.

Work together. Speak English. Good luck!

Team roles

ReaderReads the clues aloud
WriterTypes the answers

CheckerWatches the time and checks the answers

Everyone speaks English. Change roles after each clue! You have 2 help cards: press the button at the top, then your teacher or the screen gives you one hint.
